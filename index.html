// about

I'm Omkar, I'm based in Dayton and I build AI that ships.

I'm a results-driven Machine Learning and AI Engineer with 4+ years building end-to-end AI/ML solutions — spanning classical ML, deep learning, generative AI, NLP, and computer vision. Most of my recent work lives at the intersection of LLMs and production systems: building RAG applications, fine-tuning models, and standing up the MLOps that lets a model survive contact with real users.

I care as much about the retrieval and evaluation layer as I do about the model itself — a good answer that arrives three seconds too late, or can't be traced back to its source, isn't actually a good answer. That's the part of this work I find most interesting. Currently pursuing my Master's at the University of Dayton, and open to ML/AI engineering roles.
